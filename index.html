Bio

Hi! I'm Sylvia.

As a designer, I like to craft sensory joys and memorable experiences. Iterative problem-framing and integration of multiple perspectives are the must-haves for me to produce good work. Currently, I'm exploring ways to promote social sustainability with design.

My Strengths

  • Visual Design

    I'm good at creating cohesive yet flexible visual systems. Check out my Royals project and FORGE project

  • Collaboration

    Projects involving external partners (like my OnboardEase project) get me excited. I strive to understand the perspectives of the different people on my team and keep the team aligned.

  • Systematic Thinking

    While UI/UX design are user-centerd, I learned to view things from a holistic perspective from my work experience.

I love exploring things

  • Spatial Experience

    My favourite Spanish architect is Ricardo Bofill. Also check out my collection of experiential graphic on Pinterest

  • Data Visualization

    Georgia Lupi and Fan Xiang are my idols in this field.

  • Service Design

    Besides supporting the development of social service at work, I also made a short case study on Diversecity's entrepreneurship incubator program during a service design jam.
